## Platform

web

## Stack
Next.js 16 (App Router), React 19, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS v4, Framer Motion, Base UI / Shadcn primitives

## Users
- **Battery pack developers / OEMs:** Request cell characterization (EIS, Acoustic, RF spectroscopy), browse Cell Store, contact for custom battery pack prototyping.
- **EV fleet operators & OEMs:** Evaluate BatteryScope (Battery Health-as-a-Service, predictive maintenance, warranty insights) and request demo/pilots.
- **Recyclers / Second-life buyers:** Source recycled battery cells with verified State-of-Health (SoH) diagnostics from the marketplace.
- **Job candidates:** Explore engineering & R&D roles, learn about ThinkClock's mission, submit applications and track application status.
- **Investors & R&D Partners:** Review ThinkClock's R&D achievements, Innovate UK track record, leadership, and technological credibility.

## Product Purpose
ThinkClock Battery Labs is an Innovate UK–funded battery R&D company shipping advanced battery diagnostics and intelligence. The site serves as both a high-trust marketing portal and an interactive product platform featuring BatteryScope (Health-as-a-Service), CellScope (portable cell diagnostics), an interactive Cell Marketplace / Battery Configurator, and a streamlined Careers portal.

## Positioning
Deep-tech battery intelligence combining multi-physics spectroscopy (EIS, Acoustic, RF) with hardware-software digital/physical twinning for actionable battery lifetime & safety insights—setting ThinkClock apart from generic software-only BMS analytics or standard cell distributors.

## Operating Context
Web platform supporting desktop and mobile devices. Operates across commercial B2B inquiries (demo requests, custom quotes), interactive cell configuration, candidate application tracking, and administrative content management.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- **Capabilities:** Public marketing pages (Home, Technology, About, Careers, Contact), interactive Battery Configurator & Cell Marketplace (filter by voltage, capacity, new vs recycled), Account & Authentication system (saved configs, order/application status tracking), Careers application flow with resume upload, and transactional email notifications.
- **Constraints (v1):** Marketplace uses quote/inquiry flow (no live payment/checkout in v1). Live customer BMS data ingestion is out of scope for marketing site v1.

## Product Principles
1. **Science-First Credibility:** Lead with tangible battery physics and spectroscopy innovation (EIS/Acoustic/RF) over generic marketing claims.
2. **Transparent Diagnostic Intelligence:** Provide clear, traceable State-of-Health (SoH) and battery data to build confidence among OEMs and second-life cell buyers.
3. **Frictionless B2B & Candidate Journeys:** Streamline complex battery configuration and job applications into clear, interactive workflows.
4. **Clean Engineering Craft:** Ensure fast load times, responsive UI, accessibility, and robust component architecture.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Target WCAG 2.1 AA compliance with semantic HTML, keyboard navigable interactive widgets (configurator, form controls), clear visual contrast, and responsive layout across mobile and desktop.
